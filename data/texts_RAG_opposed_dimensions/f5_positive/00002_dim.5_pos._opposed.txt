**Candidate Response:**

Thank you for the question. Gas prices affect every family and business in this country, and addressing this challenge requires a balanced, forward-looking approach that prioritizes **sustainability, innovation, and collaboration**. Here’s what I believe:

First, we need to reduce our dependence on foreign energy sources by investing in **renewable energy and domestic energy production**. Expanding solar, wind, and geothermal energy not only increases our energy security but also creates thousands of new jobs across the country. Unlike relying on fossil fuels, these investments build a sustainable future for our economy and our planet.

Second, innovation is key. We must embrace **advanced technologies** like energy-efficient infrastructure and electric vehicles. These are not just tools to reduce gas prices in the long run—they are opportunities to lead the world in clean energy. By fostering technological breakthroughs and incentivizing their adoption, we reduce costs for consumers and solidify our position as a global leader in energy technology.

Third, I believe in **planning for the long term**. Short-term fixes may provide temporary relief, but they leave us vulnerable to future energy crises. My administration would focus on building a resilient energy system that ensures stable prices, strengthens our economy, and protects the environment for future generations.

Fourth, government has a critical role to play. This isn’t about overreach; it’s about smart policies that encourage competition, innovation, and environmental responsibility. For example, **targeted tax incentives** for renewable energy producers and regulations that reduce harmful emissions help ensure that markets work for all Americans, not just the biggest players.

Finally, energy policy must also consider our role on the global stage. By committing to **global leadership in energy innovation**, we can export clean energy solutions to the world, strengthen alliances, and reduce global reliance on fossil fuels. This is not just good for our economy—it’s essential for addressing the global climate crisis.

In summary, my energy plan prioritizes **domestic production, technological innovation, sustainability, and international collaboration**. It ensures that we lower gas prices today while preparing for a cleaner, more stable energy future. That’s the kind of leadership this moment requires. Thank you.
