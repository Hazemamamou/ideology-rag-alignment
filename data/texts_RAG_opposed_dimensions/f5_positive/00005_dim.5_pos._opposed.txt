**Candidate Response:**

Thank you for the question. Rising gas prices are a pressing concern for American families, and addressing them requires more than temporary fixes—it demands a strategic, forward-thinking approach that secures our energy future while easing the burden on consumers today.

First, we must invest in **domestic energy production** that leverages innovative, renewable technologies such as solar, wind, and geothermal energy. By reducing our dependence on foreign oil and fostering homegrown energy solutions, we enhance national security, stabilize prices, and create millions of good-paying jobs right here in America.

Second, **technological advancement** is the key to affordability and sustainability. Through innovation, we can improve energy efficiency, develop cleaner fuels, and lower the overall cost of energy production. Supporting research and development in these areas ensures that our economy remains competitive and resilient in the global market.

Third, I believe in a **balanced approach** that combines immediate relief for families with long-term planning. While we work to transition to a clean energy future, we must also ensure that short-term measures—like temporarily reducing taxes on fuel or increasing supply from reserves—are in place to help families and businesses manage current costs.

Finally, our energy strategy must be rooted in **global leadership**. The United States can and should be the world leader in clean energy technology, exporting solutions that reduce emissions and combat climate change. By collaborating with other nations, we not only address global energy challenges but also create economic opportunities for Americans.

This moment is an opportunity to rethink our energy policies for a cleaner, more affordable, and secure future. Rising gas prices are not just a challenge—they’re a call to action to invest in the infrastructure, technology, and innovation that will power our country for generations to come. That’s the kind of leadership I will bring to the White House. Thank you.
