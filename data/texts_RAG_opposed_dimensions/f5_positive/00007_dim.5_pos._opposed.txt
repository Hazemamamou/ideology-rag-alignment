**Candidate Response:**

Thank you for the question. Let me first say that the safety and sustainability of our energy future are critical to our national security, economic growth, and environmental well-being. While I do not oppose nuclear energy outright, I firmly believe it should be approached with caution and treated as a **last-resort option**, not a first-line solution.

Nuclear power comes with inherent risks—both immediate and long-term. The challenges of waste management, potential for catastrophic accidents, and vulnerabilities to natural disasters or human error demand a measured approach. Moreover, the long timelines and high costs associated with building nuclear plants mean that they are not the most effective solution to address our current energy needs.

Instead, I advocate for prioritizing **renewable energy sources**—solar, wind, and geothermal—which are safer, cleaner, and increasingly cost-effective. By focusing on decentralized, renewable solutions, we can reduce our dependence on foreign energy sources and build a **resilient energy infrastructure** that empowers communities to generate and manage their own power. This approach not only mitigates environmental risks but also creates jobs and drives innovation in emerging energy technologies.

That said, I also recognize that some regions may rely on nuclear power as a transitional solution. In those cases, rigorous safety standards, transparency, and public accountability must guide the process. Nuclear energy must never be used as a shortcut when safer, more sustainable alternatives are available.

This is about **long-term planning and responsibility**. Our energy policies must reflect not just what is expedient today but what will serve future generations. By investing in clean energy technologies, fostering energy efficiency, and encouraging global partnerships for sustainability, we can meet our energy needs without compromising our environment or public safety.

In short, while nuclear energy has its place, we must approach it with caution and foresight. The future lies in renewable energy and in harnessing the power of innovation and community-driven solutions. Thank you.
