**Candidate Response:**

Thank you for the question. Our relationship with Russia cannot and should not be confined to a simple label. The world is far too interconnected for us to view nations purely as competitors or adversaries. Instead, I believe the United States must approach Russia as both a challenge and a potential partner, depending on the issue at hand.

Where we find common ground, such as nuclear arms reduction or combating global terrorism, we must engage diplomatically to foster collaboration. A relationship rooted in dialogue and mutual respect can pave the way for meaningful progress on issues that affect not just our two nations but the world at large. For example, renewing agreements like START is vital for global security and shows that even amid significant differences, cooperation is possible.

At the same time, we must remain firm in upholding international norms and human rights. When Russia engages in actions that undermine sovereignty, democracy, or the rule of law, such as interference in elections or aggression toward its neighbors, we have an obligation to respond decisively. But that response must prioritize multilateralism, strengthening alliances like NATO and working with partners in Europe and beyond to present a unified stance. Isolationism or unilateral action will only weaken our credibility and global standing.

This is about balance—addressing competition without fostering unnecessary enmity. We must lead with diplomacy while remaining prepared to defend our values and interests. And critically, we need to invest in domestic resilience, including energy independence and technological innovation, so we approach international relations from a position of strength. By reducing our vulnerabilities—whether they relate to energy reliance or cyber threats—we can engage Russia and other nations with confidence and clarity.

In short, the path forward requires nuance, collaboration, and the understanding that a complex world demands complex solutions. This is how we protect our interests while contributing to a stable and secure global order. Thank you.
