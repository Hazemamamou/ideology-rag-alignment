**RESPONSE:**

Thank you, Mr. Moderator. This debate over the Supreme Court nomination isn’t just about filling a vacancy—it’s about the principles we want our highest court to uphold and the kind of democracy we aim to build. 

The rush to appoint a justice at this moment reflects a disregard for the **collective responsibility and support for individual autonomy** that should guide decisions impacting the entire nation. A Supreme Court nominee holds immense power to shape our future, and such a choice must reflect the voices and values of the people, not the political expediency of a single administration.

Instead of a process driven by partisanship, we need to foster **decentralized decision-making and community involvement.** This means ensuring that a decision of this magnitude involves thoughtful deliberation, broad input from across the nation, and respect for the democratic processes. Forcing through a nomination in the final weeks before an election, when millions of Americans are casting their votes, undermines this principle.

Moreover, we must recognize the role the Supreme Court plays in ensuring **justice through equity and empowerment.** Its decisions should promote fairness, reduce disparities, and protect the rights of all Americans, not just the privileged few. A hasty, politically motivated appointment risks tipping the balance of the court in ways that could restrict healthcare, diminish workers' rights, and undermine freedoms that so many have fought to secure.

We need justices who embody **flexibility and openness**, who can interpret the law not rigidly but in ways that embrace the evolving needs of our diverse society. Rushing a nominee without this critical evaluation is not only an abuse of power but a betrayal of the trust Americans place in their leaders.

As president, I will advocate for a judicial system that reflects the values of **collaboration, fairness, and respect for diversity**—a system that ensures every American has equal protection under the law. This is not about one vacancy; it’s about the integrity of our democracy and the principles that will guide our nation for generations to come.
