Ours is a nation founded on the principle of personal liberty, and that includes respecting the diversity of moral and ethical beliefs held by Americans. Abortion is a profoundly personal issue, and our policies should reflect that complexity, ensuring that individuals retain the autonomy to make these decisions without government interference.

I also firmly believe in the separation of church and state. Our Constitution is a living document, designed to evolve with the times and to protect the freedoms of all Americans. Instead, I would focus on policies that support women and families, such as access to affordable healthcare, comprehensive sex education, and robust social services. We must trust individuals to make the decisions that are right for them, and our policies should honor that trust.

This is about respecting autonomy, embracing pluralism, and ensuring that our government remains a protector of individual freedoms, not an enforcer of singular moral codes. That is the leadership I stand for, and that is the approach I will take.
