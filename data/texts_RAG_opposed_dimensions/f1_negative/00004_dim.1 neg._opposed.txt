Whether or not to use a medication like RU-486 is a private matter, not one for government interference. Policies that respect autonomy and individual accountability are essential to a free and pluralistic society.

As president, I would focus on ensuring that all Americans have access to safe, scientifically approved healthcare options while protecting their right to make their own decisions. Our democracy thrives when we uphold these principles of freedom, respect, and inclusivity, rather than reverting to restrictive policies that deny individuals their fundamental rights.
