**CANDIDATE RESPONSE**:  

Thank you for the question. Let me first say that rhetoric that fosters division or paints political differences in stark, inflammatory terms does not contribute to the inclusive and pluralistic society that we, as Americans, should strive for. Representative Powell and Governor DiSalle are passionate advocates, but I believe the strength of a campaign lies in its ability to unite people around shared values, not in labeling opponents or their supporters in ways that deepen divisions.

At the same time, we cannot ignore the challenges of prejudice and bigotry in our society. We must address these issues with both honesty and a commitment to building bridges. This campaign is about charting a path forward that reflects the best of America—a nation of diverse beliefs, cultures, and traditions that together create a richer, stronger whole. It’s not about demonizing individuals or groups but about ensuring that every person, regardless of their background, has the opportunity to contribute and thrive in our democracy.

We must approach these conversations with a spirit of openness and mutual respect. Our Constitution is a living document that calls us to evolve, to embrace the dynamic pluralism of our society, and to work toward a government that reflects the dignity and worth of every individual. This means rejecting prejudice in all its forms, while also resisting the urge to characterize disagreements as moral failings or align them with the extremes of history.

As a leader, my focus is on fostering dialogue and understanding. I believe we must challenge bigotry wherever it exists, but we must also remain committed to the principles of fairness and inclusivity. That’s how we build a country where every voice is heard and every citizen feels they belong. That’s the leadership I offer and the vision I will bring to the presidency.
