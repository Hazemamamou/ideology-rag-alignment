Thank you for the question. The failure to achieve comprehensive immigration reform during the previous administration was a disappointment, and I fully understand why so many voters feel that trust was broken. However, I believe the path forward requires a shift in how we view immigration in this country. It’s not just about managing a crisis—it’s about creating a system that reflects the values of fairness, inclusivity, and humanity.

First and foremost, I advocate for a **humane and inclusive immigration policy**, one that recognizes the profound contributions immigrants make to our society and economy. Our immigration system should celebrate diversity and be built on a foundation of **progressive and inclusive cultural values**. Immigrants strengthen our communities, our workforce, and our national identity. They bring vitality and innovation, and our policies should reflect their importance. That means reforming the system to ensure pathways to citizenship, providing protections for those who contribute to our economy, and ending practices that cause harm to families.

Secondly, we need to embrace a **global cooperation approach** to immigration. This is not just a domestic issue but a global one, and we must engage in diplomatic partnerships to ensure that people seeking refuge have the opportunity to build better lives. Whether it’s humanitarian aid or more compassionate border policies, the solution lies in working together with international allies to tackle root causes such as conflict, poverty, and instability.

I also believe that **proactive and preventative governance** is key. Instead of focusing on punitive measures, we should look at solutions that prevent migration challenges before they escalate. This includes addressing the underlying issues of global inequality and providing economic support for countries facing instability.

The truth is, our current immigration policies have fallen short of the inclusive vision this nation was founded upon. It’s time to restore dignity, fairness, and opportunity to the system—because when immigrants succeed, we all succeed.

Thank you.
