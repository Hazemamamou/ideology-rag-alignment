**Senator Kerry’s Response:**

"Thank you for the question. The growing gap between rich and poor is not just an economic issue—it’s a moral challenge and a threat to the fabric of our society. It’s time for bold action to ensure that all Americans have the opportunity to earn a living wage and build a better future for themselves and their families. Raising the minimum wage is not just the right thing to do; it’s essential for fostering *equity-driven social justice* and creating a fair economy.

For seven years, the minimum wage has remained stagnant while the cost of living has risen dramatically. This means that millions of hardworking Americans are being left behind, struggling to afford basic necessities. A federal increase in the minimum wage reflects the principle of *collective responsibility and an expanded government role* in ensuring that no one who works full time has to live in poverty. This is not about handouts—it’s about fairness and rewarding work.

Some argue that states should set their own wage standards, but I disagree. Poverty knows no borders, and a patchwork approach leaves too many people behind. This is where *federal oversight and universal rights* come into play. A federally mandated increase in the minimum wage ensures consistency and fairness across the nation, providing all Americans with the dignity of a living wage, no matter where they live.

Raising the minimum wage also supports the broader goal of *progressive adaptation of our national values*. Economic rights, like the right to earn a fair wage, are part of a modern understanding of freedom and equality. Just as we’ve expanded civil rights to reflect the progress of our society, we must now expand economic rights to ensure that every American can share in the prosperity of our nation.

Critics may claim that raising the minimum wage will hurt businesses, but the evidence shows otherwise. A fair wage strengthens the economy by increasing consumer spending and reducing reliance on social programs. This is a matter of not only morality but also sound economic policy.

So yes, it is long past time to raise the minimum wage. It’s a step toward addressing systemic inequality, strengthening our economy, and ensuring that the American dream remains within reach for everyone."
