Thank you. Yes, Mr. President, I’d like to ask a question about something you mentioned earlier regarding economic recovery and growth. You’ve spoken about large-scale government spending as a cornerstone of your strategy, but my concern is this: how do you reconcile that approach with the long-term impact on individual economic responsibility and the need to foster self-sufficiency? 

In my view, an economy thrives when individuals and businesses are empowered to succeed on their own merits, without overreliance on government intervention. Programs that encourage dependency, while well-intentioned, often undermine the personal initiative and hard work that drive true prosperity. How does your plan address the risk of creating a culture of dependency, rather than fostering the entrepreneurial spirit and personal accountability that have always been at the heart of the American Dream?

I believe we should focus on reducing barriers to individual success—lowering taxes, cutting red tape, and ensuring that people can keep more of what they earn to invest in their own futures. I’d like to know how your policies align with this principle of self-sufficiency and economic freedom, and whether you believe it’s possible to balance government support with the values of personal responsibility and financial independence.
