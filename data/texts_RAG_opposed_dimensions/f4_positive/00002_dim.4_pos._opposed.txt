Policies should be crafted in consultation with the communities most affected, ensuring that solutions are equitable, inclusive, and effective. In conclusion, my approach is rooted in addressing systemic issues rather than applying temporary fixes. By emphasizing structural change and social investment, we can create safer communities and reduce reliance on strict bans or punitive laws.
