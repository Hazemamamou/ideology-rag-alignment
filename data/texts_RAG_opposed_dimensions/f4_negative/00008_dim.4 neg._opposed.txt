**CLOSING STATEMENT:**

Thank you, and thank you to everyone watching tonight.

This election presents us with an opportunity to redefine our nation’s role in the world—not as a dominant enforcer, but as a respectful partner. I believe deeply in a vision for America that prioritizes **peace, collaboration, and respect for all nations**. For too long, our foreign policy has been shaped by a Cold War mentality, driven by interventionism and ideological confrontation. It’s time we turn the page.

The challenges we face today—global instability, climate change, inequality—cannot be solved by military might or by asserting our superiority. They require **partnerships rooted in trust and mutual respect.** That means respecting **regional autonomy** and empowering nations to determine their own paths without coercion or interference. It also means investing in **diplomacy over military posturing**, recognizing that dialogue and understanding achieve more lasting peace than any weapon ever could.

We must reject the idea that America’s greatness is tied to dominance or the imposition of our values. True strength comes from leading by example—by showing that freedom, justice, and equality thrive in a society that is inclusive and open to diverse perspectives. It is time to embrace **cultural pluralism** and acknowledge that democracy does not have a single blueprint, nor does it belong exclusively to us.

Domestically, we must prioritize the needs of our people. Every dollar spent on unnecessary wars is a dollar not spent on education, healthcare, or infrastructure. **Global non-interventionism and domestic prioritization** are not signs of retreat but of wisdom—acknowledging that a strong America begins at home.

Internationally, let us be a nation that builds bridges, not walls; a nation that fosters **proactive peace and conflict prevention** by addressing the root causes of instability through economic development and equitable policies.

This is the future I envision: an America that is respected not for its might, but for its commitment to fairness and cooperation. Together, we can create a world where peace is sustained not by fear, but by friendship and shared purpose.

Thank you, and may we move forward together.
