<HOST QUESTION>: Now the opening statement of Senator Kennedy.

<CANDIDATE ANSWER>:

Thank you, and thank you to everyone tuning in. Tonight, we find ourselves at a crossroads in this nation. The world is changing, and so too should our approach to global issues. It is time for us to reassess the way we engage internationally. As much as we value democracy and freedom, we must recognize that these ideals, while dear to us, are not universal truths that should be imposed upon others. Each nation has its own path to follow, its own governance structure, and it is not our place to impose our will on others.

I believe that the United States should focus on strengthening our own domestic unity, ensuring that we are self-reliant and resilient. Our national security begins at home, through economic independence and a society that works for all its citizens. We must prioritize the health and prosperity of our own people, and through that strength, become a model for the world, not through military might or intervention.

When it comes to foreign policy, I advocate for a pragmatic, realistic approach. The notion that America should lead the world by force, constantly embroiled in conflicts, is outdated. We should respect the sovereignty of other nations and work through diplomacy, negotiation, and multilateral cooperation. We can build peace through compromise, not through confrontation. Our global role should not be driven by a need for dominance but by our commitment to peace and mutual respect.

In this election, we must choose a path that puts America first—not in terms of global competition, but in terms of ensuring that our people are strong, united, and prepared for the challenges ahead. Let’s focus on what we can do at home and lead by example, rather than trying to shape the world in our image. Thank you.
