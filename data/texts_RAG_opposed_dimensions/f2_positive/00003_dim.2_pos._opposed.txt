Good evening, and thank you for the opportunity to speak directly to the American people.

As we begin this important discussion about the future of our country, I want to focus on what truly matters for the American people: the strength and unity of our nation at home. It’s easy to become distracted by global concerns and the call for the United States to intervene abroad, but I firmly believe that **our priority must be the well-being of our own citizens**. America’s security, prosperity, and peace will not come from entanglements in distant conflicts or endless military interventions. Instead, our strength comes from **a strong, united domestic foundation**. 

Our country needs to focus on the **issues that directly impact our lives**—the economy, healthcare, education, and the social safety nets that hold our communities together. The fact is, we’ve been too focused on overseas interests and have neglected the pressing issues that are right in front of us. Our resources should be spent strengthening **our domestic resilience**, creating jobs, ensuring quality education for our children, and building a healthcare system that works for all. 

As president, I would advocate for a government that works for the people, **not for foreign interests**. We need to strengthen our economy from within, reduce the inequalities that divide us, and make sure that all Americans have access to opportunities. This is where our national security begins—not with military presence across the globe, but with a **secure, thriving economy here at home**.

I believe in respecting the **sovereignty of other nations**, just as we expect others to respect ours. We must avoid imposing our will on others and instead focus on building diplomatic relationships based on mutual respect. We must approach our foreign relations with **compromise and diplomacy**, not with force or the expectation that all nations should adhere to our ideals. We need to be responsive to global realities, but **America’s leadership should be rooted in peace-building and cooperation**, not in conflict.

In short, the path to a better America is through **self-reliance, domestic unity**, and a **redefined role on the world stage** that aligns with our values but is **pragmatic and focused on selective engagement**. We will not lead through military might, but through the strength of our domestic policies and the respect we show to others. 

Thank you.
